A first piece. Cut from a deadstock workwear blank, panelled in cadmium yellow and lead grey. The jagged ears are hand-shaped over a wire armature, then sewn into the hood seam so the silhouette holds without a hat. Extra stitching runs the inner edge of each ear.

The interior carries the embroidered sigil, a quiet mark for the wearer, not for the room.

Built for the kind of weather that smells like a thunderstorm two cities over.
