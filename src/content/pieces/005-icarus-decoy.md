The body was lifted from a black blank with chlorine and a stiff brush. The yellow bat wing was cut from upholstery-grade wool felt and hand-stitched on with contrast thread.

A long, single horn — the decoy of the title — pulls focus from the wing. Both hands of the piece pointing somewhere else.

Yellow Zoo Yörk wordmark dropped wet on the front. Kept loose. Kept moving.
